## Why Luria

Most writing tools force a compromise between cluttered documents, mandatory cloud logins, or subscription locks. **Luria Novel** is built around a quieter premise: your stories stay on your own device, ready whenever inspiration strikes — online or completely offline.

- **Zero Accounts, Zero Trackers:** Start writing immediately with no sign-up and no third-party servers scanning your drafts.
- **Complete Story Workspace:** Keep your novels, chapters, worldbuilding encyclopedia, quick notes, and cover designs together in one focused place.
- **Bookstore-Grade Publishing:** Export finished manuscripts directly into formatted PDF books (across 8 trim sizes) or standard EPUB 3.0 e-books.

## How to Use Luria

### 1. Start Your Bookshelf
1. Open **[Luria Web](https://lurianovel.my.id)** or the **Luria Novel** Android app.
2. Tap **New Novel** to enter your **Novel Title**, **Author Name** (or pen name), optional **Subtitle**, and **Synopsis**.
3. Imported `.luria` stories from other writers appear neatly inside your **Imported Library**, keeping your own manuscripts separate.

### 2. Design Your Book Cover
While creating or editing a novel, choose from three cover modes:
- **Geometry:** 30 typographic cover layouts paired with 21 color palettes that automatically scale long titles and author names so text never overflows.
- **Framed:** Classic **Gallery** and **Passage** borders paired with your selected illustration.
- **Custom Image:** Upload full `2:3` artwork (recommended `600 × 900 px`) from your device.

### 3. Write in the Chapter Editor
1. Open your novel and tap **+** to add a **New Chapter** or a **New Section** (*Part 1*, *Act I*, *Volume 1*).
2. Hold and drag items to rearrange your chapter order anytime.
3. Customize your writing space:
   - **Focus Mode:** Hide peripheral bars for uninterrupted drafting.
   - **Text Appearance:** Switch among **11 literary typefaces**, adjust text size (`Small`, `Medium`, `Large`), toggle **Standard Paragraph** indentation, or set a custom editor wallpaper with blur control in **Settings**.
   - **Inline Illustrations:** Place artwork directly between paragraphs.
   - **Version History:** Preview and restore earlier saved snapshots of any chapter.
   - **Spelling & Punctuation Check:** Highlight potential typos and punctuation issues in your active chapter.

### 4. Build Your World & Capture Notes
- **Encyclopedia:** Open the **Wiki** tab to write structured lore pages with categories, images, automatic tables of contents, and novel links.
- **Notes:** Open the **Notes** tab for checklists, pinned reminders, quick scene snippets, and color-tinted cards.

### 5. Export Books for Reading & Print
1. Open a novel and choose **Download Book**.
2. Pick your output format:
   - **PDF:** Choose from **8 print and reading trim sizes**, optionally enable **Vertical** typesetting (for Japanese, Korean, and Chinese), and include a **Foreword**, **Afterword**, and **Copyright Page** (year, holder, publisher, edition, ISBN, editor, typesetter, cover designer, and notice).
   - **EPUB:** Export an **EPUB 3.0** e-book ready for Kindle, Apple Books, Kobo, and mobile readers.

### 6. Back Up & Protect Your Manuscripts
Because Luria stores your library directly on your device without cloud servers, keeping a backup protects your work when switching phones, uninstalling the app, or clearing browser storage:
1. Open **Menu** or **Settings** → **Manuscript Backup**.
2. Tap **Save Backup (.luria)** to download an encrypted archive of your novels, chapters, illustrations, Encyclopedia entries, and Notes (or export a single novel `.luria` file to share).
3. Tap **Restore Backup (.luria)** on any device running Luria to bring your library back in seconds.
4. *(Optional)* Enable **PIN Security Lock** in **Settings** for 6-digit PIN protection and encrypted local backups. Keep your PIN safe—because Luria uses no central server, forgotten PINs cannot be reset remotely.

## Publishing Formats & Typography

<details>
<summary><strong>PDF Trim Sizes (8 Standard Formats)</strong></summary>

| Format | Dimensions | Ideal Use |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A5** | `148 × 210 mm` | Standard paperback & indie print books |
| **Novel** | `140 × 200 mm` | Classic fiction & light-novel print trim |
| **UNESCO** | `155 × 230 mm` | Trade nonfiction & larger fiction editions |
| **B5** | `182 × 257 mm` | Anthologies, illustrated guides, & monographs |
| **US Trade** | `6 × 9 in` | International trade paperback print |
| **Digest** | `5.5 × 8.5 in` | Compact paperbacks & chapbooks |
| **A4** | `210 × 297 mm` | Full-page proof manuscripts |
| **A6 Pocket** | `105 × 148 mm` | Pocket editions & Bunkobon-style reading |

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Writing Fonts (11 Curated Typefaces)</strong></summary>

- **Classic & Book Serifs:** `Times New Roman`, `EB Garamond`, `Crimson Pro`, `Libre Baskerville`, `Source Serif 4`
- **Modern Literary Serifs:** `Lora`, `Literata`, `Merriweather`, `Spectral`
- **Clean Sans & Drafting Mono:** `Plus Jakarta Sans`, `JetBrains Mono`

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>East Asian Vertical Typesetting (CJK)</strong></summary>

When Luria's language is set to **Japanese**, **Korean**, or **Chinese**, authors can switch to **Vertical** (`vertical-rl`) writing and PDF publishing with support for:
- **Kinsoku Shori** line-breaking rules
- **Ruby / Furigana** annotations (`{漢字|かんじ}`)
- **Bouten** emphasis dots (`《《強調》》`)
- **Tate-chu-yoko** horizontal numerals in vertical text

</details>
