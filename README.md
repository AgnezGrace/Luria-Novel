## Why Luria

Most writing tools force a compromise between cluttered documents, mandatory cloud logins, or rigid subscription locks. **Luria** is built around a calmer premise: your stories belong on your own device, ready whenever inspiration strikes—whether you are online at a desk or offline on a flight.

- **Zero Accounts, Zero Trackers**: Start writing immediately at **[lurianovel.my.id](https://lurianovel.my.id)** without signing up or handing your drafts to third-party servers.
- **Complete Story Workspace**: Keep your novels, chapter drafts, worldbuilding wiki, quick notes, and cover designs together in one quiet space.
- **Bookstore-Grade Publishing**: Turn finished manuscripts directly into formatted PDF books (across 8 standard trim sizes) or EPUB 3.0 e-books.

## How to Use Luria

### 1. Start Your Bookshelf
1. Visit **[lurianovel.my.id](https://lurianovel.my.id)** in your browser (or open the installed app).
2. Select **New Novel** to enter your **Novel Title**, **Author Name** (or pen name), optional **Subtitle**, and **Synopsis**.
3. Use **Imported Library** whenever you open a `.luria` story shared by another writer so borrowed works stay separate from your own manuscripts.

### 2. Design Your Book Cover
1. While creating or editing a novel, pick from three cover modes:
   - **Geometry**: 30 modern typographic covers paired with 21 color palettes that automatically resize long titles and author names so text never overflows.
   - **Framed**: Classic **Gallery** and **Passage** borders paired with your chosen illustration.
   - **Custom Image**: Upload full 2:3 artwork (recommended `600 × 900 px`) directly from your gallery.

### 3. Write in the Chapter Editor
1. Open your novel and tap **+** to create a **New Chapter** or a **New Section** (such as *Part 1*, *Act I*, or *Volume 1*).
2. Hold and drag chapters or sections to arrange your reading order at any time.
3. Inside the editor, customize your comfort and tools:
   - **Focus Mode**: Hide peripheral bars for uninterrupted writing.
   - **Text Appearance**: Switch among **11 literary typefaces**, adjust text size (`Small`, `Medium`, `Large`), enable **Standard Paragraph** indentation, or set a soft-blurred custom editor wallpaper in **Settings**.
   - **Inline Illustrations**: Insert artwork cleanly between story paragraphs.
   - **Version History**: Review and restore earlier saved snapshots of a chapter whenever you revise a scene.
   - **Spelling & Punctuation Check**: Run a manuscript check on your active chapter and accept or dismiss suggestions inline.

### 4. Build Your World & Capture Notes
- **Encyclopedia**: Switch to the **Wiki** tab to create structured articles with sections, automatic tables of contents, reference images, categories, and links to related novels.
- **Notes**: Switch to the **Notes** tab for quick drafts, scene checklists, pinned reminders, color-tinted cards, and novel tags.

### 5. Export Books for Reading & Print
1. Open a novel and choose **Download Book**.
2. Select your output format:
   - **PDF**: Pick from **8 print and reading trim sizes**, optionally toggle **Vertical** book layout (available for Japanese, Korean, and Chinese), and add your **Foreword**, **Afterword**, and **Copyright Page** (year, holder, publisher, edition, ISBN, editor, typesetter, cover designer, and notice).
   - **EPUB**: Generate a standard **EPUB 3.0** e-book ready for Kindle, Apple Books, Kobo, and mobile reading apps.

### 6. Back Up & Protect Your Manuscripts
Because Luria stores all data directly on your own device rather than on a cloud server, periodic backups keep your work safe when switching phones, clearing browser storage, or reinstalling:

1. Open **Menu** or **Settings** → **Manuscript Backup**.
2. Tap **Save Backup (.luria)** to download an encrypted archive of your novels, chapters, illustrations, Encyclopedia pages, and Notes (or export a single novel `.luria` file to share with readers or move between devices).
3. Tap **Restore Backup (.luria)** on any device running Luria to bring everything back in seconds.
4. *(Optional)* Turn on **PIN Security Lock** in **Settings** to protect your device workspace and `.luria` backups with a 6-digit PIN. Be sure to store your PIN safely—because Luria has no central server, forgotten PINs cannot be reset remotely.

## Publishing Formats & Typography

<details>
<summary><strong>PDF Trim Sizes (8 Standard Formats)</strong></summary>

| Format | Dimensions | Ideal Use |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A5** | `148 × 210 mm` | Standard paperback & indie print books |
| **Novel** | `140 × 200 mm` | Classic Indonesian & light-fiction novel trim |
| **UNESCO** | `155 × 230 mm` | Trade nonfiction & larger fiction editions |
| **B5** | `182 × 257 mm` | Anthology, illustrated guide, or monograph |
| **US Trade** | `6 × 9 in` | Standard international paperback print |
| **Digest** | `5.5 × 8.5 in` | Compact trade paperback & chapbooks |
| **A4** | `210 × 297 mm` | Full-page proof manuscripts & portfolios |
| **A6 Pocket** | `105 × 148 mm` | Pocket editions & Bunkobon-style reading |

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Writing Fonts (11 Curated Typefaces)</strong></summary>

- **Classic & Book Serifs**: `Times New Roman`, `EB Garamond`, `Crimson Pro`, `Libre Baskerville`, `Source Serif 4`
- **Modern Reading & Literary Serifs**: `Lora`, `Literata`, `Merriweather`, `Spectral`
- **Clean Sans & Drafting Mono**: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, `JetBrains Mono`

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>East Asian Vertical Typesetting (CJK)</strong></summary>

When Luria's language is set to **Japanese**, **Korean**, or **Chinese**, authors can toggle **Vertical** (`vertical-rl`) writing and PDF publishing with support for:
- **Kinsoku Shori** line-breaking rules
- **Ruby / Furigana** annotations (`{漢字|かんじ}`)
- **Bouten** emphasis marks (`《《強調》》`)
- **Tate-chu-yoko** horizontal numerals inside vertical columns

</details>

## Privacy & Authorship

- **100% Ownership**: Every title, chapter, illustration, encyclopedia page, note, cover design, and exported book belongs strictly to you. Luria claims zero rights or royalties over your work.
- **No AI Training or Ad Tracking**: Luria does not scan your stories, show ads, or upload your library for model training.
- **True Offline Reliability**: Once loaded in your browser, your shelf, editor, wiki, notes, and PDF/EPUB exporters continue working without an internet connection.
